Serial Console Quick Reference

Print-friendly cheat sheet for baremetal UART debugging on BBAI

BBAI Serial Pinout

SignalHeaderPinUSB-UART Wire
UART0 TXP924 (pin 11)RX (white)
UART0 RXP926 (pin 13)TX (green)
GNDP91 (pin 1)GND (black)

UART0 is the default U-Boot console. Your kernel uses UART3 at 0x49020000 — you'll need a second UART or reconfigure to UART0.

PRCM UART3 Clock Control

RegisterAddressBitsDescription
CM_L4PER_UART3_CLKCTRL0x4A009B281:00=DISABLE, 1=ENABLE, 2=AUTO

Write 2 (AUTO) or 1 (ENABLE) to this register before touching UART3. On reset it may be 0, causing silent TX drops.
